## Typography

Euodos uses **Google Sans Flex** as its logotype and primary typeface. This wiki uses it for all text.

## Logos

Variations: *Wave* (the wave alone), *stacked* (wave above the name) and *strip* (wave beside the name). Each comes in a light version (for dark backgrounds) and a dark version (for light backgrounds).

### Do

- Use the logos when **linking to Euodos** or **mentioning it** in articles and posts.

### Don't

- Use the logos to brand **your own** website, app, ROM or other products.
- Imply an association with or endorsement by Euodos.
- Warp, transform or otherwise manipulate the logos beyond what is recommended.
