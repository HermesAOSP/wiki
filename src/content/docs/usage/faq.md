<details>
<summary>Does Euodos work with banking apps?</summary>

It depends on the app and the integrity level it requires. Without certified Google services, some apps may refuse to run.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I go back to the stock ROM?</summary>

Yes, by flashing the manufacturer's official firmware. Remember to back up your data: this wipes the device.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does unlocking the bootloader void the warranty?</summary>

Often, yes. Check your manufacturer's terms before you start.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I report a bug?</summary>

Follow the [Reporting bugs](/development/reporting-bugs/) guide.

</details>
